# death of the wikiblogardenite

I'm writing a lot less on my blog and it's because i'm commuting a lot less. I'm working at home more often. I still commute into London sometimes.

I'm not sure about blog posts anyway. Prose is really boring and saps a lot of energy out of a story. 

If I was still writing on my blog here's what i would write about: 

## Organising as an art practice

I think you can make anything an art practice / medium. One of my favourites to watch is "organising". When I think of this, there are two people that spring to mind for me. 

one person is alex mclean, and at events he runs i enjoy watching and observing how he quietly gets people involved, subtly gently. like at errorcamp recently making space for people in the live scoring (for example). also bringing some weaving to us in London at the recent algorave decelerate. but yeah.. at errorcamp it was wonderful just admiring it from across the room, i enjoyed noticing the little things he was doing. it really is a craft, in more ways than one. 

another person is daniel from the London scene who is infamous for his infamous "world record worst live coding set ever" performances. he's extremely bold and provocative as an artist with these practices!! and even before he did this, i enjoyed being surprised and inspired by his various "live learning" performances, like the one he did with me in the science gallery. I'll always remember that. i really respect an artist who does what they think is right artistically, regardless of what's expected of them, often breaking expectations. and of course the intention here is to lower barriers and standards in the community, and it bloody works! it's both an artistic statement and a practical measure. (it's nice when those things match up). so yeah, his recent performance at algorave decelerate was awesome to watch because it was so daniel in the sense that he made so much space for people around him, NK, shaan, miki, and it felt like a unified people, with chau bringing in the melanated-coalition-inspired amapiano-inspired visuals

i'm trying to improve my organise-as-art-practice skills. i'm doing this by practising and trying new things

## I am queer

i am queer. i am queer as fuck! i continue to reflect on what that means for me, my place in the world and all. what it means for a very non-binary person of my flavour of non-binary to be doing the things i'm doing. i still experience huge misunderstanding almost constantly from both outside and inside the queer "community" though i don't really think there is a queer "community", only localised patches.

i don't think we're intersectional enough. i want to get more intersectional on every level. i don't want to be at a "queer event" or a "queer community" i want to go to an "event" i want to be part of a "community" where straight and queer people are all mixed up. i want us to all understand each other and i want people to understand me. i don't feel like I'm "out" of the closet because the world doesn't know the truth about me, even though i tell them. at conferences and events people guess the wrong things all the time with me, but it's not only that.

## Daily bars

been writing daily bars as part of the algorave London community, helped by nkosi. he gave me a lot of wisdom on writing bars a while back and said some really interesting things about algogrime on a (currently still being edited) episode of my docu series algotours

one thing i like about "bars" is.. nkosi said "bars" is a loose word. it could mean some bars that you rap, or poetry, or spoken word, or something you sing, or even a mantra.. maybe this is bars? hmm probably not

either way, I've experienced myself getting better and quicker at writing bars of various types by doing it every day. it's a very more-ish activity. I've been focusing on flow and flow changes, and SPEED, as in.. i'm trying to make myself faster at writing them. I've begun to be able to speak very bad bars in realtime, on the spot, which is a really lovely and strange feeling. it feels like i lose control of the words coming out of my mouth. it's very surrealist!! my favourite surrealist artist, ted joans, did poetry and i think this is one of the reasons i am so keen to learn to do bars 

i want to thank the [melanated coalition](https://www.instagram.com/melanatedcoalition?stkn=azhsZGtoajV5Zjlp) for teaching me these things and for making space for me

## i don't share everything

i guess i am occasionally reminded that i need to remind people that i don't share *everything* on the internet. the mantra is "normalise sharing scrappy fiddles" not "normalise sharing everything", but many people seem to interpret it in that way, for some strange reason. 

if your experience and assessment of me is only based on things you can perceive over the internet, you probably have a very strange perception of me

## The tech worlds

the chaos caused by the introduction of AI is still rocking the tech world(s) and i don't think it will land for a while. it does feel like it's going to a different stage now, though. at least that's what i hear from people at conferences. what i notice is that it's a very taboo subject that is talked about constantly, despite being a taboo subject(!!) which is a recipe for disaster. AI is a *political* topic, something the tech worlds are grossly unprepared for. i don't think it ever will. i really do think that tech conferences should try to get more than just engineers and business people to its conferences though. we should be bringing designers and "product people" along too, i think. it was really nice to see so many scientists at GOTO this year! they brought great perspectives. also really appreciated the educator perspective that was there too
