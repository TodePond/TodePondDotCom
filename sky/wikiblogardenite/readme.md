# The wikiblogardenite turns three years old

My website is three years old now! Here's the annual birthday post, following on from [last year's tradition](https://www.todepond.com/go/two/), and the [one before that](https://www.todepond.com/wikiblogardenite/one/year/).

I've actually stopped posting things on my wikiblogardenite. I determined that text really sucks as a medium. So I decided to do [NO MORE TEXT](https://www.todepond.com/sky/no-more-text/).

Thinking back to when I first started [My wikiblogarden](https://www.todepond.com/wikiblogarden/my-wikiblogarden) back in 2023... Two of the people I list as influences for this website are now fully AI-pilled. So I now wonder if they're not good people to be influenced by. I still really like all those people, but I now wonder if I shouldn't let them influence my computer practice. 

I mean, as time goes on I think about doing less and less on the computer, full stop. No feelings, no nothing. The computer is toxic. 

The things I make or put on the computer should self destruct. They should make you close the computer. In this sense, they should be built *not* to last. I call it: Impermacomputing. Because there's one thing worse than computers breaking and that's computers sticking around. 

That does mean losing more contact with people over the internet but maybe that's a good thing anyway.

I'll delete my wikiblogardenite soon. Save any post you want to keep!
